arthop

A free planner for Amsterdam Art Week, Wednesday 18 to Sunday 22 November 2026.

  • The whole programme on one map, day by day: exhibitions, openings, talks and tours.
  • Each day has its own opening hours; what's closed shows in grey.
  • Add what you like and your walk draws itself, with walking times between stops.
  • Plan with friends: react to picks, decide together, split into crews.

No account needed. Unofficial fan planner, not affiliated with Amsterdam Art Week. A project by Arian Shafa.
